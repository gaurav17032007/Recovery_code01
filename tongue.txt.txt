Peter Piper picked a peck of pickled peppers, but while he picked them, a quick quirky
 queen quietly questioned why Peter picked peppers instead of perfect purple plums.
 Meanwhile, a big black bug bit a big black bear, making the bear bleed black blood as 
the bug boldly buzzed by. Near the noisy river, a lazy lizard slowly slid and silently
 slipped while seven slippery snakes swiftly slithered south. She sells seashells by the 
seashore, but the shells she sells are surely seashells, and the seashore shines sharply
 in the sun. If two witches were watching two watches, which witch would watch which watch
 while the wind whispered wildly? As the clock ticked and the thick stick struck the stone,
a smart student steadily spoke strong sentences to train his tongue and strengthen his
 speech.
